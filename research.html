Research and Quality

ORIC supports research and innovation. The QEC looks after quality and feedback.

ORIC

Office of Research, Innovation and Commercialization

ORIC was set up on the directive of the Higher Education Commission and was recognised by HEC in 2022 in the highest category. It strengthens research, industry links, patents and commercialization.

QEC

Quality Enhancement Cell

  • Promote public confidence that quality and standards of awards are protected.
  • Review the quality of teaching and learning in each subject area.
  • Define the qualifications framework and set standards for degrees.
  • Develop quality assurance methods and procedures, including program monitoring and student feedback.
