Department of Computer and Software Technology

Academic Block A, Main Campus. Programs in artificial intelligence and data science.

Aims

To provide education and training at all levels, so that graduates can join the national pool of computer scientists and meet the needs of industry and academia.

Objective

Computing has shaped the information technology revolution and a large software industry. The department builds research and teaching in this field to serve that demand.

BS (Artificial Intelligence)

Builds deep knowledge to turn large and complex problems into decisions. Covers computing, mathematics, automated reasoning, statistics, machine learning, natural language processing and vision, with courses on ethics and social responsibility.

Offered by DCST

BS (Data Science)

Combines the basic principles of statistics and computer science. Builds foundations in algorithms, data structures, data management and machine learning for careers in data analysis.

Offered by DCST
