About the University

A public-sector university serving the people of Swat and the whole country.

The valley

Rooted in the Valley of Swat

The valley is known for green forests, clear rivers and mountain scenery, and is often called the Switzerland of the East. The university takes its name and its spirit from this place.

History

How the university began

From an announcement in 2010 to a campus with thousands of students.

  1. 29 May 2010The establishment of the University of Swat was announced during the then Prime Minister of Pakistan's visit to the Swat Valley.
  2. July 2010The university came into formal existence by order of the Governor of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  3. August 2010Dr. Muhammad Farooq Khan (Shaheed) was appointed the first Vice Chancellor. Two buildings at Saidu Sharif were handed over.
  4. 2010-11The first academic session began with 7 departments, which later grew to 27.
  5. TodayThe Main Campus is at Charbagh, with a Women Campus and the Shangla Campus established as sub-campuses.
Vision and mission

What we stand for

The university was announced in May 2010 and began in July 2010. Phase-I includes three academic blocks, hostels for girls and boys, a masjid, library, examination section, administration block and staff residences. Phase-II is planned for the near future.

To be a leading center of excellence in higher learning and research, dedicated to nurturing intellectually capable, morally upright, and globally competitive leaders who serve the nation with distinction.

To deliver market-relevant, quality education through rigorous academic standards, cutting-edge research, and innovation, while fostering ethical leadership and addressing regional and global socioeconomic challenges.

Integrity, Academic Excellence, Inclusivity, Ethical Conduct, Innovation, and Social Responsibility in service to society.

1. Provide accessible, modern higher education across disciplines.
2. Promote impactful scientific and applied research.
3. Expand modern digital campus infrastructure and industrial linkages.
4. Cultivate civic responsibility and sustainable community development.

Aspiring for Quality Education with Relevance
