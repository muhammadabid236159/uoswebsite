How to apply

Five steps from policy to enrollment

1

Read the policy

Study the admission policy and prospectus for eligibility and rules.

2

Choose a program

Check undergraduate, MS/MPhil and doctorate programs by department.

3

Apply when announced

Watch the announcements for the admission schedule and apply online.

4

Check the merit list

Merit lists are posted on the homepage and under announcements.

5

Complete enrollment

Submit the required documents and fee as instructed in the notice.
